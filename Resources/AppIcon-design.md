### Initial Generation
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one final production-ready macOS application icon for PhotoCatalog, a professional local RAW photo catalog and photography library workbench. This is the actual icon asset, not a presentation, mockup, app screenshot, or logo sheet.
Output: one square 1024 x 1024 PNG with genuine alpha transparency around the outer icon silhouette. No checkerboard pattern or solid background outside the silhouette.
Design: a centered gently rounded-square base in light silver porcelain, matching a light-gray professional workspace. Subtle precise bevel, restrained soft shadow, beautifully finished but not toy-like. The base occupies roughly 88 percent of the canvas with balanced transparent margins.
Main symbol: a compact stack of three photographic slides, generously sized and centrally composed. Two pale silver back slides are slightly offset upward and sideways, with only their edges visible, clearly communicating an organized photo library. The front slide is almost upright, with a thick clean graphite photographic mount. Its image is a striking simplified alpine landscape: ivory mountain peaks, a teal lake, clear cobalt sky, and a small warm coral sun. Use bold coherent planes and just enough natural photographic texture to read as photography, not an overly detailed scenic painting. Four substantial cobalt-blue viewfinder corner marks are integrated into the front mount, expressing selection and curation.
Palette: neutral silver #E8E9EB and soft white #F4F5F6 base, graphite #1C1D1F mount, confident restrained blue #2764D6 accents, teal landscape, a small coral highlight. Crisp light-dark separation and strong recognizable silhouette at 32 and 64 pixels. Premium, quiet, precise, native macOS utility character. Controlled shallow dimensionality, front-facing orthographic composition.
No letters, initials, words, numbers, watermarks, rainbow flower, camera body, aperture wheel, busy contact sheet, hairline details, translucent glass blob, ornamental sparkles, external scene, giant cast shadow, extra icons, or alternate versions.
