PhotoCatalog 是一款 Mac 上的照片管理和 RAW 修图应用。

PhotoCatalog is a photo manager and RAW editor for the Mac.

免费开源，用法接近 Lightroom Classic：把照片导入目录库，选片、加关键词，非破坏性地修 RAW，再导出、打印或做成画册。照片在你的 Mac 上处理，不需要账户，也没有订阅。

It's free and open source, and works in the style of Lightroom Classic: import photos into a catalog, cull and keyword them, develop RAW files without changing the originals, then export, print or make a book. Your photos are processed on your Mac, with no account or subscription.

选片Culling

  • 网格、单张、比较和筛选视图；筛选视图可以把一组连拍并排对比。
  • 数字键评分，P / X 标为精选或拒绝，可以设置成评分后自动跳到下一张。
  • RAW+JPEG 合并为一张显示，连拍按拍摄时间自动叠放。
  • 智能相册、相册集和快捷收藏。
  • Grid, loupe, compare and survey views; survey puts a burst side by side.
  • Rate with the number keys and pick or reject with P and X, optionally moving to the next photo after each.
  • RAW+JPEG pairs show as one photo, and bursts stack by capture time.
  • Smart albums, album sets and a Quick Collection.

RAW 修图Developing

基于 macOS 自带的 RAW 引擎。修图保存为一组参数，原始文件不会改变。包括配置文件与黑白、色调曲线、混色器、颜色分级、LUT、细节、镜头校正、镜头模糊、透视校正和软打样。每一步都记在历史记录里，预设可以和 Lightroom 互相导入导出。

Development uses the RAW engine built into macOS, and edits are saved as settings, so the original file never changes. There are profiles and black and white, tone curve, color mixer, color grading, LUTs, detail, lens corrections, lens blur, Upright and soft proofing. Every step goes into the history, and presets import from and export to Lightroom.

蒙版Masks

主体、天空、人物、物体和景观蒙版由 App 自带的模型在本机计算，另外还有渐变、画笔、颜色范围和明亮度范围蒙版。AI 移除、降噪和超分辨率也在本机运行。

Subject, sky, people, object and landscape masks are computed on your Mac by models that ship with the app, and there are gradient, brush, color range and luminance range masks too. AI removal, denoise and super resolution also run locally.

整理Organizing

  • 关键词、相册和智能相册，可以按日期、相机和镜头筛选。
  • 本机人脸识别，在 LFW 基准上的准确率为 99.4%。
  • 地图视图，以及按 GPX 轨迹匹配拍摄位置。
  • 查找完全相同和相似的照片。
  • 评分、关键词和标题写入 XMP 附属文件，Lightroom 和 Bridge 都能读取。
  • Keywords, albums and smart albums; filter by date, camera and lens.
  • Face recognition on your Mac, 99.4% accurate on the LFW benchmark.
  • A map view, and locations matched from GPX tracks.
  • Finds exact and similar duplicates.
  • Ratings, keywords and titles go into XMP sidecars that Lightroom and Bridge read.

输出Output

导出支持预设、输出锐化和水印；打印时使用打印机的色彩配置文件；幻灯片可以存成 MP4 视频；还能生成网页画廊，或排版 PDF 画册并逐页预览。

Export with presets, output sharpening and watermarks. Print through your printer's color profile. Save a slideshow as an MP4 video, build a web gallery, or lay out a PDF photo book and preview it page by page.

常见问题

Common questions

PhotoCatalog 是免费的吗？

Is PhotoCatalog free?

是。PhotoCatalog 免费开源，以 GPL-3.0 许可发布；不需要账户，没有订阅，也没有内购。即将上架的 Mac App Store 版同样免费。

Yes. PhotoCatalog is free and open source under the GPL-3.0, with no account, subscription or in-app purchases. The upcoming Mac App Store edition is free too.

支持哪些 Mac？

Which Macs does it run on?

搭载 Apple 芯片（M 系列）、运行 macOS 14 Sonoma 或更高版本的 Mac。不支持 Intel 芯片的 Mac。

Macs with Apple silicon (M-series) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.

我的照片会被上传吗？

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

不会。目录库、预览和修图记录都在你的 Mac 上，人脸识别、AI 蒙版和降噪也在本机运行。只有使用可选的 AI 助手、查看地图或检查更新时才会联网，详见隐私政策。

No. The catalog, previews and edits stay on your Mac, and face recognition, AI masks and denoise run on it. The app goes online only for the optional AI assistant, maps and update checks; see the privacy policy.

能和 Lightroom 一起用吗？

Does it work with Lightroom?

不能直接打开 Lightroom 目录库（.lrcat），但会读写 Lightroom 和 Bridge 都能识别的 XMP 附属文件（评分、颜色标签、关键词、标题、说明和位置），修图预设也能以 .xmp 文件导入和导出。

It can't open Lightroom catalogs (.lrcat), but it reads and writes the XMP sidecars Lightroom and Bridge use (ratings, labels, keywords, titles, captions and locations), and it imports and exports develop presets as .xmp files.

支持哪些 RAW 格式？

Which RAW formats does it open?

佳能（CR2、CR3）、尼康（NEF）、索尼（ARW）、富士（RAF）、奥林巴斯 / OM System（ORF）、松下（RW2）、宾得（PEF）、三星（SRW）和 DNG。RAW 由 macOS 自带的 RAW 引擎解码，所以某款相机能否打开取决于 macOS 是否支持它。另外支持 JPEG、HEIC、PNG、TIFF、GIF、WebP、BMP，以及 MOV、MP4、M4V 视频。

Canon (CR2, CR3), Nikon (NEF), Sony (ARW), Fujifilm (RAF), Olympus / OM System (ORF), Panasonic (RW2), Pentax (PEF), Samsung (SRW) and DNG. RAW files are decoded by the RAW engine built into macOS, so a camera works once macOS supports it. JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF, GIF, WebP and BMP open too, as do MOV, MP4 and M4V videos.

会改动原始照片吗？

Does it change my original files?

不会。修图只记录在目录库里，导出时生成新文件。评分、关键词等元数据可以写入原片旁边的 XMP 附属文件，原片本身保持不变。

No. Edits are recorded in the catalog, and exports are new files. Metadata such as ratings and keywords can be written to XMP sidecars beside your originals; the originals themselves are left as they are.

隐私Privacy

App 没有账户、统计或追踪，也不会自行上传你的照片库。只有使用 AI 助手、查看地图或检查更新时才会联网；如果用的是本机的 AI 服务，照片不会离开你的 Mac。

The app has no account, analytics or tracking, and never uploads your library on its own. It goes online only when you use the AI assistant, view a map or check for updates, and with an AI service running on your Mac, your photos don't leave it.
